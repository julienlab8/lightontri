Registration

Register for an event.

Registration is online only, at lightontri.com, for Woippy**, Pierre-Percée*** or Madine***. Opens Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 8:00 PM (Paris time) - the "Register" buttons stay disabled until then.

2027 prices

L***: €119 (licence holder) / €134 (non-licence holder). M**: €59 / €69. S*: €34 / €39. Kids: €10.

Licence & medical certificate

Licence holders: triathlon licence to be uploaded on the registration platform. Non-licence holders: a medical certificate of no contraindication to competitive triathlon, less than a year old, to be submitted at least 15 days before the event.

Bib collection

The day before each race, from 17:00 to 19:00, or on race day depending on the event's schedule. Photo ID required.

Changing your plans

A bib transfer is possible up to 48h before the first collection slot (€3). A 50% refund on presentation of a medical certificate up to one month before the event, or a cancellation pack taken out at registration (€15 on L***, €10 on M**, not offered on S*).

First triathlon

Never done a triathlon ?

Swimming, cycling and running on the same day: it sounds daunting before you try it, and it's simpler to prepare for than you'd think. Here's what you need for a calm start to your first LIGHT ON TRI.

The transitions

Between the swim and the bike, then between the bike and the run, you pass through your transition zone (bike park). Scout your spot and the flow of traffic before the start - that's where you save precious minutes, without the stress.

The wetsuit

Mandatory if the water is not above 16°C, recommended in all cases. The swim cap provided by the organiser, on the other hand, is always mandatory. Try your wetsuit in open water before the big day if it's your first time.

The S* format, the right way in

500 m swim, 20 to 25 km bike, 5 km run: the S* format is designed for a first triathlon, with no time-limit pressure.

Drafting and finer race rules: the 2027 rules don't yet detail these points - they'll be covered in each event's participant guide.
Gear & race rules

What to have on you.

A written briefing is sent by email a few days before each event. In the meantime, the essentials of the 2027 rules.

Bib & timing chip

The bib provided must not be cut down or altered - only one is issued per participant. The timing chip is worn on the left ankle and must be returned after the finish, or a €50 charge applies.

Bike

A sticker provided by the organiser must be attached to your helmet and to the seat post of your bike.

Swim

The swim cap provided by the organiser is mandatory. A wetsuit is mandatory if the water is not above 16°C, recommended in all cases.

Feed stations

No outside feeding is allowed other than what's provided by the organiser.

Environment

Respect for the environment is required at all times on the course, under penalty of immediate sanction.

Time limits

L***: swim cut-off at 1h10, bike cut-off at 5h30. M**: bike cut-off at 3h00. S*: no time limit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a licence to take part?

Licence holders: your triathlon licence is to be uploaded on the registration platform. Non-licence holders: a medical certificate of no contraindication to competitive triathlon, less than a year old, to be submitted at least 15 days before the event.

What's the minimum age to take part?

S*: from the Cadet category upwards (born 2011 or earlier). M** and L***: from the Junior category upwards (born 2009 or earlier). Mini-Kids: born 2018, 2019, 2020 or 2021. Kids: born 2014, 2015, 2016 or 2017.

How do I register?

Online only, at lightontri.com - no on-the-day registration. 2027 registration opens Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 8:00 PM (Paris time).

I can no longer make it, what are my options?

A bib transfer is possible up to 48h before the event's first bib collection slot (€3). On presentation of a medical certificate, 50% of the registration fee is refunded up to one month before the event. No refund within the last month, unless a cancellation pack was taken out at registration (€15 for L***, €10 for M**, not offered on S*). Full details in the rules.

Do I need a wetsuit?

It's mandatory if the water temperature is not above 16°C, and recommended in all cases. The swim cap provided by the organiser is always mandatory.

Are there prizes?

Yes, on L*** (€200/150/100 to the top 3 men and women) and M** (€100/75/50), plus the top 3 male and female veterans on each format. No prize on S*.

Are there time limits?

L***: swim cut-off at 1h10, bike cut-off at 5h30. M**: bike cut-off at 3h00. S*: no time limit.
