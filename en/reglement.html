2027 Rules The rules.

LIGHT ON TRI event rules, last updated 07/10/2026. By registering, you accept these rules - take five minutes to read them.

Article 1 · Events

The LIGHT ON TRI events for the 2027 season are:

  • LIGHT ON TRI Woippy**: Sunday 16 May 2027.
  • LIGHT ON TRI Pierre-Percée***: Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 June 2027.
  • LIGHT ON TRI Madine***: Saturday 18 and Sunday 19 September 2027.

The Madine*** date was communicated directly by the organiser; the rules published on 07/10/2026 still listed "date to be announced" for this event at the time of writing.

Article 2 · Briefing and distances

A written briefing will be sent by email a few days before the event.

Wearing a wetsuit is mandatory if the water temperature is not above 16°C. We recommend wearing one to take part in our events regardless. The athlete must wear the swim cap provided by the organiser.

The timing chip must be worn around the left ankle during the event and returned after crossing the finish line. Any chip not returned will incur a €50 charge.

A sticker must be attached to the helmet and to the bike's seat post. The bib provided must not be cut down or altered. It may be worn on a race-belt fixed at three points. Only one bib is issued per participant.

No outside feeding is permitted other than that provided by the organiser. All athletes must respect the environment at all times, under penalty of immediate sanction.

The formats offered vary by event, as follows:

  • S* distance: approximately 0.5 km swim - 20 to 25 km bike - 5 km run.
  • M** distance: approximately 1.5 km swim - 40 to 45 km bike - 10 km run.
  • L*** distance: approximately 1.9 km swim - 85 to 90 km bike - 21 km run.
  • Mini-Kids and Kids duathlons.
  • Standalone run, depending on the event.

The XS distance announced for Pierre-Percée*** in the rules published on 07/10/2026 is ultimately not being offered - confirmed directly by the organiser.

Article 3 · Categories

  • S* Triathlon: from the Cadet category upwards (born 2011 or earlier).
  • M** Triathlon: from the Junior category upwards (born 2009 or earlier).
  • L*** Triathlon: from the Junior category upwards (born 2009 or earlier).
  • Mini-Kids: born 2018, 2019, 2020 or 2021.
  • Kids: born 2014, 2015, 2016 or 2017.
  • 6 or 7 km run: depending on conditions specific to the event.

Age categories and eligibility conditions remain subject to the applicable federation regulations in 2027.

Article 4 · Registration

Registration is online only, at www.lightontri.com.

  • L*** Triathlon: €119 (licence holder) / €134 (non-licence holder).
  • M** Triathlon: €59 (licence holder) / €69 (non-licence holder).
  • S* Triathlon: €34 (licence holder) / €39 (non-licence holder).
  • Kids: €10.

Licence holders must upload their triathlon licence on the registration platform. Non-licence holders must upload a medical certificate of no contraindication to competitive triathlon (or sport), less than a year old, on the registration platform at least 15 days before the event.

The prices, competition passes and supporting documents applicable are those shown on the event's registration platform on the day of registration.

Article 5 · Bib collection

Bib collection takes place the day before each race from 17:00 to 19:00, or on race day, depending on the event's schedule. Photo ID is required to collect a bib.

Article 7 · Swim

LIGHT ON TRI Woippy**

The swim start is in the water: M** Triathlon, 2 laps of 750 metres (Australian exit); S* Triathlon, 1 lap of 500 metres.

LIGHT ON TRI Pierre-Percée***

The swim start is on the beach: L*** Triathlon, 2 laps of 950 metres (Australian exit); M** Triathlon, 2 laps of 750 metres (Australian exit); S* Triathlon, 1 lap of 500 metres. Details of the youth duathlons will be shared in the participant guide.

LIGHT ON TRI Madine***

The swim start is on the beach: L*** Triathlon, 2 laps of 950 metres (Australian exit); M** Triathlon, 2 laps of 750 metres (Australian exit); S* Triathlon, 1 lap of 500 metres.

The organiser reserves the right to change the courses.

Article 8 · Bike

LIGHT ON TRI Woippy**

The bike course starts from the bike park at the Woippy Plage site: M** Triathlon, 3 laps; S* Triathlon, 1 lap.

LIGHT ON TRI Pierre-Percée***

The bike course starts from the bike park at the Pierre-Percée site. The 2027 routes and number of laps are still being finalised and will be detailed in the participant guide.

LIGHT ON TRI Madine***

The bike course starts from the bike park at the Lake Madine site (Nonsard-Lamarche side): L*** Triathlon, 2 laps; M** Triathlon, 1 lap; S* Triathlon, 1 lap.

The organiser reserves the right to change the courses.

Article 9 · Run

LIGHT ON TRI Woippy**

The run course starts from the bike park at the Woippy Plage site: S* Triathlon, 2 laps; M** Triathlon, 3 laps.

LIGHT ON TRI Pierre-Percée***

The run course starts from the bike park at the Pierre-Percée site. The 2027 routes and number of laps are still being finalised and will be detailed in the participant guide.

LIGHT ON TRI Madine***

The run course starts from the bike park at the Lake Madine site (Nonsard-Lamarche side): L*** Triathlon, 3 laps; M** Triathlon, 2 laps; S* Triathlon, 1 lap.

The organiser reserves the right to change the courses.

Article 10 · Prizes

L Triathlon: top 3 men / top 3 women; top 3 male veterans / top 3 female veterans. M Triathlon: top 3 men / top 3 women; top 3 male veterans / top 3 female veterans. S Triathlon: top 3 men / top 3 women.

  • L - 1st: €200 (men) / €200 (women). 2nd: €150 / €150. 3rd: €100 / €100. 4th and 5th: free entry for the N+1 edition.
  • M - 1st: €100 (men) / €100 (women). 2nd: €75 / €75. 3rd: €50 / €50. 4th and 5th: free entry for the N+1 edition.

No prizes on the S formats.

Article 11 · Image rights

Each athlete authorises LIGHT ON TRI's organisation to use images in which they may be identifiable, for promotional and advertising purposes. In accordance with the French Data Protection Act of 6 January 1978 and applicable regulations, each participant has a right of access to and rectification of their personal data.

Article 12 · Insurance and liability

LIGHT ON TRI is covered by SMACL Assurances under policy no. C2020-3654. The organisers cannot be held liable for theft, loss or accidents resulting from poor health or non-compliance with the rules.

The organiser accepts no liability for health risks incurred by a participant as a result of taking part in the event. The participant acknowledges that taking part carries serious risks, including the risk of death, and that they are solely responsible for ensuring their state of health allows them to take part.

The participant consents to receiving any necessary medical care and treatment during the competition. Treatment costs are not included in the registration fee. It is the participant's responsibility to take out any insurance necessary or useful for this purpose. The participant is solely responsible for their personal belongings and sporting equipment.

The participant acknowledges that they may encounter vehicles or pedestrians on the course and accepts the risks associated with these circumstances. Risks include, among others, falls, collisions, technical incidents, equipment failure, inadequate protection, and risks related to ground conditions, spectators, volunteers or weather.

The participant agrees to familiarise themselves with the course and transition areas before the event. Taking part constitutes acceptance of the course and transition areas as set.

For insurance reasons, the bib is personal and non-transferable outside the transfer procedure set out in Article 15. Anyone taking part under a third party's bib without a proper transfer will not be covered by any insurance in the event of an accident.

Article 13 · Cancellations and refunds

On presentation of a medical certificate, the organiser will refund 50% of the registration fee, excluding fees charged by the registration platform, up to one month before the event. The amount of any day insurance will be refunded in full.

No refund will be given for any other reason for withdrawal. No refund is possible within the month preceding the event. Registration transfer remains possible under the conditions of Article 15.

In the event of cancellation for a cause not attributable to the organiser - including natural disaster, force majeure, security requirements, weather conditions or administrative decision - no registration can be refunded and no damages can be claimed from the organiser.

Article 14 · Cancellation pack

  • L*** Triathlon: €15.
  • M** Triathlon: €10.

The cancellation pack allows for a full refund of the registration fee up to three months before the event, then 50% of the amount from three months to one month before the event, less the cost of the pack and excluding platform fees. No refund is possible within the month preceding the event.

For non-licence holders, the day insurance is refunded in full. No cancellation pack is offered on the S formats.

Article 15 · Bib transfer

A bib transfer is possible up to 48 hours before the event's first bib collection slot, at a cost of €3. The original holder must go to their runner area, select "transfer my registration" and enter the new participant's email address. The new participant must then complete a new registration with payment. The original holder will be refunded within eight days to the account linked to the card used for payment.

Bibs are not transferable to the following year. The organiser cannot carry out this transfer on behalf of the participant.

Article 16 · Time limits after the start

  • L*** Triathlon: swim cut-off at 1h10; bike cut-off at 5h30.
  • M** Triathlon: bike cut-off at 3h00.
  • S* Triathlon: no time limit.
