Swim, bike, run

Three disciplines, one site.

In-water swim start at Woippy Plage. Bike and run both start from the same bike park.

Swim

M**: 2 laps of 750 m (Australian exit). S*: 1 lap of 500 m.

Bike

Starts from the bike park at Woippy Plage. M**: 3 laps. S*: 1 lap.

Run

Starts from the same site. S*: 2 laps. M**: 3 laps.

Village LIGHT ON

Everything happens at the Village LIGHT ON.

Bib collection, starts, finishes and transitions: it's all centralised at the Woippy Plage site.

Bib collection

Photo ID required. Time slots detailed in the schedule above.

Prize ceremony

Prize money on M** only (€100/75/50), men and women, plus veterans. No prize on S*.
