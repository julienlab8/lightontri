S*

The format to get started: an accessible distance across all three disciplines, with no time limit to worry about.

Swim0.5 km
Bike20-25 km
Run5 km
Price€34-39
Register for the S*

Who's it for?

Your first triathlon, getting back into it, or wanting to try all three disciplines without aiming for performance.

Categories & certificate

Open from the Cadet category upwards (born 2011 or earlier). A medical certificate less than a year old is mandatory, to be uploaded at least 15 days before the event.

Time limit

No time limit on the S*.

Offered at

Woippy**, Pierre-Percée*** and Madine***.

M**

The next step up: real time on the bike, a transition that needs practice and a distance that needs preparing for.

Swim1.5 km
Bike40-45 km
Run10 km
Price€59-69
Register for the M**

Who's it for?

For those ready to step up, with real time on the bike and a transition that matters.

Categories & certificate

Open from the Junior category upwards (born 2009 or earlier). A medical certificate less than a year old is mandatory, to be uploaded at least 15 days before the event.

Time limit

3h00 on the bike.

Offered at

Woippy**, Pierre-Percée*** and Madine***.

L***

The big format, for triathletes comfortable over distance and ready to spend several hours racing.

Bike85-90 km

Register for the L***

Who's it for?

The big format, for triathletes comfortable over distance who want to go big.

Categories & certificate

Open from the Junior category upwards (born 2009 or earlier). A medical certificate less than a year old is mandatory, to be uploaded at least 15 days before the event.

Time limit

1h10 on the swim, 5h30 on the bike.

Offered at

Pierre-Percée*** and Madine***.

Family format

Kids & RUN 7 KM

At Madine***, younger athletes have their own races - Mini-Kids (born 2018-2021) and Kids (born 2014-2017), priced at €10 - and everyone can join the RUN 7 KM, a standalone run not connected to the triathlon. Not yet confirmed for Woippy** and Pierre-Percée*** - to be checked.
