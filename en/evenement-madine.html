Madine*** · 18-19 September 2027 S*, M**, L***: the grand finale.

The last date of the season, by Lake Madine: S*, M**, L*** triathlons, plus Kids and RUN 7 KM on the same weekend.

Register See the events
Note: the hour-by-hour 2027 Madine schedule is not yet published - it will be shared in the event's participant guide. The photos on this page are from the 2026 edition (full gallery and testimonials on results and photos); they illustrate the atmosphere by the lake, not the 2027 schedule.
Swim, bike, run

Three disciplines, one lake.

Beach swim start, on the Nonsard-Lamarche side. Bike and run both start from the same bike park.

Swim

L***: 2 laps of 950 m (Australian exit). M**: 2 laps of 750 m (Australian exit). S*: 1 lap of 500 m.

Bike

Starts from the bike park on the Nonsard-Lamarche side. L***: 2 laps. M**: 1 lap. S*: 1 lap.

Run

Starts from the same site. L***: 3 laps. M**: 2 laps. S*: 1 lap.

Kids & RUN 7 KM: two formats offered at Madine, alongside the triathlons. 2027 route and timing details to come in the participant guide.
Village LIGHT ON

Everything happens at the Village LIGHT ON.

Bib collection, starts, finishes and transitions: it's all centralised at the Lake Madine site for the weekend.

Bib collection

Photo ID required. 2027 timings to be confirmed in the participant guide.

LIGHT ON Shop

The LIGHT ON shop sets up at the Village for the weekend.

Prize ceremony

Prize money on L*** (€200/150/100) and M** (€100/75/50), men and women, plus veterans. No prize on S*.
