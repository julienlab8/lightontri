Swim, bike, run

Three disciplines, one lake.

Beach swim start for all three numbered formats. Bike and run both start from the bike park at Pierre-Percée.

Swim

L***: 2 laps of 950 m (Australian exit). M**: 2 laps of 750 m (Australian exit). S*: 1 lap of 500 m.

Bike

Starts from the bike park at Pierre-Percée. 2027 routes and lap counts still being finalised - detailed in the participant guide.

Run

Starts from the same bike park. 2027 routes and lap counts still being finalised - detailed in the participant guide.

Youth duathlons: route details will be shared in the participant guide, not yet published.
Village LIGHT ON

Everything happens at the Village LIGHT ON.

Bib collection, starts, finishes and transitions: it's all centralised at the Pierre-Percée site for the three days.

Bib collection

Photo ID required. Each format has its own slot, detailed in the schedule above.

LIGHT ON Shop

The LIGHT ON shop sets up at the Village for the three days.

Prize ceremony

Prize money on L*** (€200/150/100) and M** (€100/75/50), men and women, plus veterans. No prize on S*.

Pierre-Percée & the Vosges

Lake Pierre-Percée.

The Village LIGHT ON sets up on the shore of Lake Pierre-Percée, in the Vosges (88110). Exact address, access and parking: to be confirmed in the participant guide.

The formats

S*, M** and L***, plus Mini-Kids and Kids. For full distances, head to the formats.

Getting there & staying overnight

Access, parking and accommodation around the lake: to be found in practical info once 2027 details are confirmed.
